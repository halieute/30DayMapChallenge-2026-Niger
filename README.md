# 30DayMapChallenge 2026: Mapping Fisheries, Water, and Biodiversity in Niger

## Overview

This repository contains my contributions to the #30DayMapChallenge 2026.

The project focuses on the spatial analysis and visualization of fisheries, aquatic ecosystems, biodiversity, water resources, and environmental processes along the Niger River and other aquatic ecosystems of Niger.

Throughout November 2026, I will create one map per day following the official themes of the #30DayMapChallenge.

## Objectives

- Promote the use of GIS and cartography in fisheries management.
- Showcase environmental and biodiversity datasets from Niger.
- Explore creative cartographic techniques.
- Develop reproducible geospatial workflows.
- Share open geospatial resources with students, researchers, and practitioners.

## Study Area

The Republic of Niger, with particular emphasis on:

- Niger River
- Major fisheries
- Wetlands and floodplains
- Aquatic biodiversity hotspots
- Fishing communities
